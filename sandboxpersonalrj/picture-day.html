Why schools book The Foundry

  • Zero cost to the school. No fees, no minimums, no contracts to sign.
  • We handle everything. Scheduling around your classes, photographing every child, collecting orders, delivering prints.
  • Parents order online. A QR code on the order form takes them to your school's ordering page. Your staff never touches money or forms.
  • Free class photo for every classroom, every season.
  • Free retake day for absences and the smiles that got away.
  • A local owner, not a call center. The person who answers your email is the person holding the camera.

How it works

  1. Pick a date. We find a morning that fits your class schedule — even part-time programs. Two dates held on request, no contract.
  2. Picture day. We photograph every child, plus a class photo. Order forms go home with a QR code; parents order and pay online.
  3. Delivery day. Prints arrive at your school in about two weeks, sorted by child. Digital images go straight to parents by email.

Meet your photographer
